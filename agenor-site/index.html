Frequently asked questions

The short answers to what most Bermuda shippers ask.

Do I need a full container? +

No. We consolidate — send a single pallet or a partial load and we combine it with other Bermuda-bound cargo. Full containers are available too when you have the volume to fill one.

How long does it take? +

About a week. On our most-used service (Bermuda Container Line) cargo sails Friday with an official ETA in Bermuda of Monday; allow roughly one extra day for customs clearance.

When is the cargo cut-off? +

Wednesday end of day for that week's Friday sailing (Thursday morning is sometimes possible). Documents — a commercial invoice, plus a dangerous-goods declaration if applicable — are due by cut-off.

Where do I drop off my cargo? +

At our Ontario partner warehouse — Polimex Warehouse, 27 Selby Road, Brampton. Tell us before you send it so we can expect it and issue your reference number, and label every piece with that number. If you'd rather, we can arrange trucking to collect it from you.

What can I ship? +

Commercial freight, household and personal effects, vehicles, and oversized or project cargo. Dangerous goods can be handled with the proper declaration.

Do you handle customs and paperwork? +

Yes. We prepare and manage the documentation so your shipment clears smoothly on arrival.

How do I get a quote? +

Email your shipment details — origin, what you're shipping, rough size and weight, and the destination in Bermuda — or call us, and we'll come back with a price and a plan.
